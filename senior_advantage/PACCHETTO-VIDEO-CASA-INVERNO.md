Over 60? 9 Kinds of Help You Can Get at Home This Winter (Most Never Ask)

## Descrizione
Over 60 and need help at home this winter? There are 9 kinds of help that can come to your home, and most seniors never ask for them. In this video: smoke alarms installed in your home by the American Red Cross, homemaker help, chore help for the heavy jobs, hot meals brought to your door, grab bars and home repairs, legal help for housing and scams, a break for family caregivers, protection from a winter heat shutoff, and help when your furnace stops working.

The one call to remember: the Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116 (call or text). Say: "I'm over 60, I live in [your city], and I need help at home this winter. Please connect me to my Area Agency on Aging."
