1 Costco does not have a senior discount. Not at sixty, not at sixty five, not at any age. And every month, thousands of people search for it anyway.
2 I checked Costco's own website. The answer is clear. But I also found twelve things inside Costco that really do save seniors money, and most of them need no coupon. Stay to the end, because number twelve is the one almost everyone gets wrong.
3 First, the truth. Basic membership costs sixty five dollars a year, and Executive costs one hundred thirty. It is the same price for every age. Costco's own customer service page says it does not offer discounted memberships for seniors.
4 So where does the saving come from? From services already built into Costco that most seniors never ask about. Here they are, one by one.
5 One. The pharmacy. You do not need a membership to fill a prescription at a Costco pharmacy, online or in the warehouse. Members get the member price. Non members can still pay with cash, a debit card, a shop card or a Visa.
6 So if you are not a member, you can still use it. And if you are a member, keep watching, because the next one is even bigger.
7 Two. The Member Prescription Program. It is not insurance. It is a discount program, and Costco says it can lower some drug prices by up to eighty percent or more, depending on the medication.
8 There is nothing to sign up for, and no extra fee. You just show your Costco card at the pharmacy. It also works at thousands of participating pharmacies, not only at Costco.
9 Before you go, look up your medication on Costco's website, costco dot com slash c m p p, and compare the price. Your own savings depend on the drug.
10 Three. The hearing aid center. The hearing test is free for members, and it is done in a sound booth in about an hour.
11 Costco says the price you see is the price you pay. Your hearing aids come with a free warranty period that varies by model, plus free follow up visits, cleanings and check ups, and loss and damage coverage with no deductible.
12 Get the test first, then compare prices. Models and prices change, so confirm everything at the hearing aid center near you.
13 Four. The eye exam. You do not need a membership to book an exam with the independent optometrist at or near most Costco warehouses.
14 But to buy glasses or contact lenses, you do need a membership. So you can have the exam first, and decide about the glasses after.
15 Five. Free tech support for what you buy there. If you buy a television, a computer, a printer, a camera or a major appliance at Costco, you get free technical support from a service called Concierge.
16 The phone number is one, eight six six, eight six one, zero four five zero. It is open every day, from five in the morning to eight at night, Pacific time.
17 Have your membership number, the item number from your receipt, the model, the serial number and the purchase date ready. So keep that receipt.
18 Six. A second year of warranty. On televisions, projectors, computers and major appliances, Costco extends the manufacturer's warranty to two years from the day you bought it.
19 It follows the manufacturer's coverage. If something is not covered in year one, it is not covered in year two. And touchscreen tablets are not included. So before you pay for an extended warranty anywhere, check what you already have.
20 Seven. Tires. When you buy tires at Costco, installation is included, and so is maintenance for the life of those tires: rotation, balancing, pressure checks and flat repairs.
21 There is also a five year road hazard warranty. It only applies to tires you bought at Costco, so keep your paperwork.
22 Eight. Money back when the price drops. If something you bought goes down in price within thirty days, Costco can refund you the difference.
23 For online orders, the credit usually comes back in five to ten business days. Keep your receipt, and ask at the membership desk.
24 Nine. The return policy. Costco calls it a risk free, one hundred percent satisfaction guarantee on membership and merchandise, with a few exceptions.
25 Some electronics have a ninety day limit: televisions, computers, tablets, cameras and cell phones. So do not wait. And always read the exceptions on Costco's website.
26 Ten. A second card at no extra cost. Your membership comes with one free household card, for someone over sixteen who lives at your address.
27 That could be your spouse, or an adult child who lives with you. Two people can shop, and you pay for one membership.
28 Eleven. Vaccines at the pharmacy. Costco's pharmacists give many C D C recommended vaccines, including the flu shot, covid boosters, shingles and pneumonia vaccines.
29 You do not need to be a member to use the pharmacy, and you can walk in or book through the Costco app. Ask the pharmacist about the price, and whether your insurance covers it.
30 Twelve. The Executive membership, and here is the honest math. It costs sixty five dollars more than the basic card.
31 In return, you get a two percent reward on eligible purchases, up to one thousand two hundred fifty dollars a year. Sixty five divided by two percent is three thousand two hundred fifty. That is how much you must spend in a year just to break even.
32 And Costco itself says the reward is not guaranteed to be equal to or greater than the upgrade fee. If you spend less than that, stay with the basic card. Do the math with your own receipts.
33 So Costco has no senior discount. But a pharmacy, a prescription program, a hearing test, an eye exam, tech support, a second year of warranty, tire care, price adjustment, easy returns, a free household card and vaccines can add up.
34 Here is the one rule to remember. Never pay for an extra before you check what already comes with what you own.
