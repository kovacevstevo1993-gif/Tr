## Descrizione
Black Friday return and price match rules: I read the official policy pages of Walmart, Target and Costco, not blogs or coupon sites. One of the pages has a Black Friday exception written on it, and at one store the return clock for some holiday electronics doesn't start until December 26.

This is not about a senior discount. It's about how long you have to return something, and what happens when the price drops after you buy. At two of the three stores you only have a limited number of days to ask for a price match.

Watch the full video below: store by store, with what each page itself says.
