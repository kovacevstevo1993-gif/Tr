What's Target's price match policy?
Value at Target is about feeling a sense of satisfaction that you paid the right price without sacrificing experience, quality or style, no matter when in the season you decide to shop.
We remain focused on simplifying the shopping experience to provide great deals when and where our guests want to shop.
Target's Price Match Guarantee
Starting November 1, 2026, if you purchase an item in store or online and the price goes lower at Target on or before December 24, 2026, you can request a price match. 
Learn more about Target's holiday price match policy.
We’ll match the price if you buy a qualifying item at Target and then find the identical item for less at Target.com, in Target stores or with a Target Circle™ deal (deals that apply automatically upon identifying yourself as a Target Circle member). Price matches may be requested at time of purchase or within 14 days after purchase. 
To request a price match for purchases made in store, provide proof of the current lower price at Guest Services in your local Target store. For Target.com or Target app purchases,
chat with us online
 or call Guest Services at 1-800-591-3869.
Reference the details below for more information on what items are eligible for price matching and what proof is required.
Guidelines & Limitations
The price for an item must be listed and valid at the time of match. If Target is not able to verify the lower price, we may decline the request. 
The item must be the identical item, brand name, size, weight, color, quantity and model number. 
Proof of any Target Circle deal requires member sign in, sign up or mobile number entered at checkout.
Please show the online price or digital version of the ad on your mobile device for proof of price; Target team member will verify. 
Original receipts, physical or digital, or packing slips are required for price adjustments up to 14 days after purchase.
In-store price matches from other Target stores are excluded.
When price matching with the Target app, the Target app must be showing the price of the current store location.
We match Target.com prices on in-store Target purchases and in-store Target prices for Target.com purchases. 
Price match of items sold by Target.com or prices through the Target app are not available in Alaska and Hawaii.
We reserve the right to deny purchase and limit quantities per guest.
Exclusions
Target Circle Bonuses (just-for-you bonuses that you must select to add in the Target app or on Target.com), gift card offers (Target GiftCard, specialty and pre-paid gift card), Registry completion coupons and Target coupons cannot be combined with price match. Manufacturer coupons will be applied after a price match is made, for in-store purchases only. Please review our coupon policy for additional details.
Clearance, closeout, liquidation sales, damaged, used, open package, refurbished, pre-owned, or rent / lease to own items, discount on total store or site purchase, deals that require a minimum purchase, non-branded items.
Pricing due to typographical errors.
All credit card offers, gift card offers financing, service offers, bundled offers, sales tax promotions, free items, rebates or mail-in offers.
Contract mobile phone devices and the associated plans, optical, product services (warranties, assembly, etc.), clinic and pharmacy purchases and offers, or pre-order items.
Items containing alcohol will be subject to state-by-state regulations.
Target items cannot be price matched to Target Plus items.

What's the return policy?
Target reserves the right to deny returns, refunds and exchanges including but not limited to prevent fraud, suspected fraud or abuse.
For purchases November 1 through December 24, 2026, there's an extended return window for electronics and entertainment, including Apple® & Beats® products and most Target Plus items. 
Learn more about the holiday extended return window.
For all other items, the normal Target and Target Plus Partner return policy applies.
Target's Return Policy
Most unopened items sold by Target in new condition and returned within 90 days will receive a refund or exchange. The return window for items sold on Target.com begins once the order has been invoiced. Invoicing happens when any part of the order is shipped, delivered by a Shipt shopper or made ready for pick up. Some items sold by Target have a modified return policy noted on the receipt, packing slip, Target policy board (refund exceptions), Target.com or in the item description. Items that are opened or damaged or do not have a receipt may be denied a refund or exchange. 
Visit here for information on return exceptions.
Target Plus™ Partner’s Return Policy
Most unopened items sold by Target Plus in new condition and returned within 30 days will receive a refund. The return window for items sold on Target.com begins once the order has been invoiced. All items sold by Partners must be returned to Target either in store or by mail. Some items sold by a Target Plus Partner have a modified return policy on Target.com. Items that are opened or damaged may be denied a refund. In-store returns without an eligible receipt will be denied. Some items may have a shorter return window. Refer to the 
Shipping & Returns
 section of the individual item’s description or the 
Return Exceptions
 section.
Visit here to learn how to start your return.
