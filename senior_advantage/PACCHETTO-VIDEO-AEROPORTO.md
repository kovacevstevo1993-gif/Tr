Flying Over 60? Airports and Airlines Owe You These 8 Things (If You Ask)

## Descrizione
Flying over 60? At airport security there is a rule written only for travelers 75 and older, and almost nobody at the checkpoint says a word. In this video you'll see 8 things airports and airlines offer when you ask: your REAL ID, help from the curb to your gate, your cane and walker, notice for oxygen and CPAP, TSA Cares, the 75+ screening rule, your medications at the checkpoint, and your refund when a flight is cancelled. Every point comes from official TSA pages and federal airline rules.

Honest note: most airline assistance rules are based on disability, not on age. The only age rule in this video is the TSA screening for travelers 75 and older.

The one rule to remember: being allowed to do something doesn't change what happens at the airport. Only asking does. Say it out loud.
