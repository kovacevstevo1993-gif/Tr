7 Bills Seniors Over 60 Can Lower Before Winter (Most Keep Paying Full Price)

## Descrizione
Most seniors over 60 keep paying 7 bills in full that a government program can help lower. In this video you'll see the 7 bills, who each one helps most, and the right order to apply so you don't miss help you already qualify for: heating (LIHEAP), energy (Weatherization), phone (Lifeline), groceries (snap), the monthly food box (CSFP), half-price bus rides, and a home repair grant of up to $10,000 (USDA Section 504). Number 7 is the biggest.

The one rule to remember: being eligible doesn't lower a single bill. Only the application does, and nobody will fill it out for you.
