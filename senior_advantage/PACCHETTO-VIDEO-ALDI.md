Aldi Has No Senior Discount (Aldi Says So). 10 Things To Know Before You Shop

## Descrizione
Is there an Aldi senior discount? Aldi's own help center says it does not offer discounts at its store locations. So I read Aldi's help pages and found 10 real rules, fees and policies that can save you money, time or a wasted trip: the 25-cent cart deposit you get back, the day new Aldi Finds start, what Aldi does and doesn't accept as payment, gift cards, what ordering online really costs, and the Twice as Nice Guarantee.

The one habit that protects you on every Aldi trip: keep your receipt until you've checked it, and until the product is gone. Aldi says a refund to your original form of payment needs a receipt; without one you get an Aldi merchandise credit at the item's current price, which may not match what you paid.
