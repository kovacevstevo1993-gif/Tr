## Titolo
Watch This Before You Buy on Black Friday: Return and Price Match Rules at Walmart, Target, Costco

## Descrizione
Black Friday return policy and price match rules: what Walmart, Target and Costco say on their own official pages, and the dates to write on your receipt before you buy.

Do you know the exact last day you could return what you buy on Black Friday? I read the official policy pages of Walmart, Target and Costco and put the return windows and the price match rules side by side. That includes the Black Friday exception written on Walmart's own price match page, the Target holiday return and price match dates, and Costco's 90 day rule for electronics.

The one habit that protects you: keep every receipt (for online orders, the order number) and write the return deadline on it, using the dates on that store's own page.

What the official pages say, in short:
Walmart: 90 days for most items. Items bought October 1 through December 31 are returnable until January 31, with exceptions. Hearing aids, prescription glasses and contact lenses are listed at 60 days. Walmart's price match page does not match walmart.com Black Friday or Cyber Monday deals.
Target: electronics and entertainment items (except Apple and Beats) bought November 1 through December 24, 2026 can be returned until January 24, 2027. Apple, Beats and unlocked or prepaid mobile phones: until January 8.
Costco: 90 days for electronics. For items with sale dates, such as holiday promotions, a price match request must be made within both the promotion dates and 30 days of the purchase.

Page dates: Walmart's return policy was updated October 9, 2026 and its price match section June 8, 2023. Costco's return page was published October 2025 and its price match page May 2026. Target's holiday page is the 2026 one.
