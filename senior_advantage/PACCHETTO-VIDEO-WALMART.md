## Descrizione
Is there a Walmart senior discount? I searched Walmart's website and its official pages, and I couldn't find one. But Walmart will give you 10 other things if you know how to ask: it matches its own website price, holiday returns until January 31, free curbside pickup ($35 minimum, per Walmart), a flu shot with no out-of-pocket cost on most major insurance plans (per Walmart), SNAP online, Walmart+ Assist at half price ($49 a year, if you qualify), prescription delivery, 10 cents off a gallon of gas at participating stations with Walmart+, the $4 prescription list (not offered in North Dakota) and a 90-day supply of some generics for $10.

The one rule that works at every counter: ask before you pay. At the pharmacy, say: "Is my medicine on your $4 or $10 list, and would the cash price be lower than what I pay with my plan?"
