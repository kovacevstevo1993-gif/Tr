In the next few minutes you will see what Walmart, Target and Costco say on their own policy pages about returns and price matching, including one Black Friday exception written right on Walmart's page. 2 First, where this comes from. Everything in this video was read on the official policy pages of Walmart, Target and Costco, and I tell you the date on each page when it shows one. I did not use blogs or coupon sites. Also, this video is not about a senior discount. It is about two things that protect your money at any age: how long you have to return something, and what happens if the price drops after you buy.
3 Why do these two things matter on Black Friday? Because a gift bought on Black Friday may not be opened until Christmas morning, and a thirty day return window that started on Black Friday is almost gone by then. Prices also move during the sales, so a price match can matter too. Every store handles both in its own way, and the details are in the fine print. So let's go one store at a time, starting with Walmart.
4 Walmart first. Walmart's return policy page says you have ninety days after purchase or upon receipt to exchange or return an item, unless it is listed in the exceptions. You show your store receipt, or your order number for something bought online, and Walmart refunds your purchase to the way you paid. You can return in the store, curbside, free by mail, or schedule a pickup with FedEx. The page I read was updated on October ninth, twenty twenty six.
5 What if you lost the receipt? Walmart says it can attempt to look up store purchases with a debit or credit card, and online orders with a phone number or email. Now the holiday rule. Under Extended Holiday Returns, Walmart says most items bought between October first and December thirty first are returnable until January thirty first. Walmart adds that some exceptions may apply, such as wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, Dyson products, and select seasonal and luxury goods.
6 Now the shorter windows, which matter for electronics. Walmart's page lists major appliances at two days after delivery, with up to thirty days for unopened, uninstalled ones in factory sealed packaging. Most wireless phones get fourteen days. Consumer electronics and personal computers get thirty days. Hearing aids, prescription glasses and contact lenses get sixty days. The page doesn't spell out how the holiday rule applies to each, so ask a Walmart associate before you buy.
7 Next, Walmart price matching. It has its own section, titled Walmart Price Match Policy, and that section was last updated on June eighth, twenty twenty three, so keep that date in mind. For an item you buy in a Walmart store, Walmart will match the price of the identical item advertised on walmart dot com. You must tell the associate the price at checkout, and the identical item must be in stock and available to buy on walmart dot com at that moment.
8 Now the part about Black Friday. In its list of what it does not match, the page names walmart dot com prices on special events, including clearance, Rollback, Black Friday or Cyber Monday deals, and other limited time promotions. It also doesn't match competitor prices. The store manager on duty has the final decision. For purchases made on walmart dot com, the page says it offers no price matching, including for prices that dropped after you bought.
9 Now Target. Target's holiday page for twenty twenty six says purchases made between November first and December twenty fourth get an extended return window, and that window begins on December twenty sixth. Electronics and entertainment items, except Apple and Beats, get thirty days from then, so they must be back by January twenty fourth, twenty twenty seven. Apple and Beats products and unlocked or prepaid mobile phones get fourteen days, so the deadline is January eighth.
10 For everything else, the normal Target policy applies. The page says most unopened items sold by Target, in new condition and returned within ninety days, will receive a refund or exchange. It also says items that are opened or damaged, or that have no receipt, may be denied a refund or exchange. The page mentions extended return windows for Target Circle Card holders and Target Circle three sixty members, but it doesn't list the days here, so check the return policy page for that detail.
11 Target also has a holiday price match. If you buy an item in a Target store or online starting November first, and the price goes lower at Target on or before December twenty fourth, you can request a price match. Target says you can ask at the time of purchase or within fourteen days after. In a store, you bring proof of the lower price to Guest Services. For Target dot com or app purchases, you chat online or call Guest Services. Original receipts, paper or digital, are required.
12 Read the limits too. The item must be identical, with the same brand, size, color, quantity and model number. Target says a price match cannot be combined with Target Circle bonuses, gift card offers, or Target coupons. It excludes clearance, closeout, open package and refurbished items, and deals that require a minimum purchase, among others. Target can limit quantities per guest, and price matching for Target dot com and the app is not available in Alaska and Hawaii.
13 Now Costco. Its return policy page, published in October twenty twenty five, calls it a Risk Free one hundred percent Satisfaction Guarantee. Electronics have their own rule. Costco accepts returns within ninety days from the date you received the item for televisions, projectors, major appliances, computers, tablets, smart wearable devices, cameras, drones, camcorders, M P three players and cellular phones. For phones, return details vary by carrier service contract.
14 Costco also lists items it will not refund: airline and live performance event items, precious metals, Shop Cards, and gift cards. Custom special order items cannot be returned or refunded, except for warranty repair or replacement. For hearing aids, the page says to contact the Hearing Aid center at your Costco warehouse. And products with a limited useful life, like tires and batteries, may be sold with a product specific limited warranty.
15 Costco's price match page was published in May twenty twenty six. For items with specific sale dates, such as holiday savings promotions, a price match request must be submitted within both the active promotional dates and thirty days of the purchase date. Requests submitted after the promotion has ended may be denied. For other purchases, an item that drops in price within thirty days of your purchase is eligible. And Costco says it does not price match with other businesses or retailers.
16 A few more details from that Costco page. It is titled for Costco dot com orders. For a warehouse purchase, it tells you to ask at the Returns counter of the Costco where you bought the item. For Costco dot com, credits are typically issued within five to ten business days, to the way you originally paid. Costco says it can deny a request at its sole discretion, and that it may restrict its price match policy in the future, with any restrictions shown at the point of purchase.
17 Let's recap, store by store. Walmart: ninety days for most items, a holiday rule to January thirty first, shorter windows for phones, appliances and electronics, and a price match policy that excludes walmart dot com Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals. Target: extended returns for purchases from November first to December twenty fourth, and a price match request within fourteen days, with receipts. Costco: ninety days for electronics, and price matches within thirty days and the sale dates.

If your item is on a shorter list, like phones, appliances or hearing aids, ask at the counter first. And if you want a price match, ask before you leave the store, because Target and Costco give you a limited number of days, and Walmart does not match walmart dot com Black Friday deals.
