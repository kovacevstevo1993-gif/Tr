# VIDEO 10 - Medicare 2027 Changes - FONTI UFFICIALI (verificate 15/10/2026)
1. medicare.gov fact sheet "Your Medicare in 2027: What's New & Changing" (CMS Product No. 12229, September 2026), https://www.medicare.gov/publications/12229-your-medicare-in-2027-whats-new-and-changing.pdf :
   - GLP-1 Bridge: certain GLP-1 drugs for weight loss, if you have Part D, aren't already getting these drugs covered by Part D, and meet other requirements: $50 copayment for a one-month supply (Medicare.gov/glp1bridge).
   - TrumpRx coupons and other discount cards (used instead of Medicare coverage) are not creditable coverage and do NOT count toward the Medicare deductible or out-of-pocket maximum.
   - Electronic prior authorizations: starting January 1, 2027, MA plans may process prior authorization electronically.
   - Telehealth at any location in the U.S., including home, through December 31, 2027.
   - Negotiated prices: first 10 Part D drugs from January 1, 2026; next 15 Part D drugs take effect January 1, 2027. "Contact your plan for details on how these negotiated prices might affect you."
   - Open Enrollment October 15 - December 7; compare at Medicare.gov/plan-compare.
2. medicare.gov "How much does Medicare drug coverage cost?": Part D deductible max $700 in 2027 ($615 in 2026); out-of-pocket threshold $2,400 in 2027 ($2,100 in 2026); after that $0 for covered Part D drugs for the rest of the year. Medicare Prescription Payment Plan spreads costs over the year, "doesn't save you money or lower your drug costs".
3. CMS press release 28/09/2026 "Medicare Advantage and Medicare Prescription Drug Programs Expected to Remain Stable in 2027": OE Oct 15 - Dec 7, 2026; average MA premium $14.37 (2026) -> $12.00 (2027), -16.5%; stand-alone Part D $35.09 -> $36; MA drug premium after rebates $11.32 -> $7 (-38%); MA plans 5,553 -> ~5,532; ~8 in 10 MA enrollees can keep current plan at same or lower premium; MA enrollment ~34 million (47.4%).
4. CMS fact sheet "Negotiated Prices for Initial Price Applicability Year 2027": 15 Part D drugs, effective January 1, 2027; 30-day price 2027 vs 2024 list: Ozempic/Rybelsus/Wegovy $274 vs $959 (-71%); Trelegy Ellipta $175 vs $654 (-73%); Linzess $136 vs $539 (-75%); Breo Ellipta $67 vs $397 (-83%); Tradjenta $78 vs $488 (-84%); Janumet $80 vs $526 (-85%); Xtandi $7,004 vs $13,480 (-48%). About 5.3 million of 53 million Part D enrollees used these drugs in 2024; estimated $685 million out-of-pocket savings for enrollees in 2027 (standard benefit).
5. medicare.gov "Joining a plan": Open Enrollment Oct 15 - Dec 7, coverage starts January 1; Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment January 1 - March 31 (switch MA plan or go back to Original Medicare + drug plan).
6. medicare.gov "Plan Annual Notice of Change (ANOC)": your plan sends it each fall (typically September), lists changes to costs and coverage that take effect in January.
7. CMS 2026 (video 8 FONTI): Part B premium 2026 $202.90/month, deductible $283. 2027 Part B premium: not announced yet (15/10/2026).
