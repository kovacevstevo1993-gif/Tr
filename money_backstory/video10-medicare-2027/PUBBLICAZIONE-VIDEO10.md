## Descrizione
Medicare 2027 changes are now confirmed, and Medicare open enrollment 2027 ends on December 7. One decision in the next few weeks sets what you pay for Medicare all of next year. We go through the 5 new rules with the official numbers from Medicare and CMS, step by step.

We follow Frank and Mary, two fictional neighbors. Frank is 66, on Original Medicare with a Part D drug plan, and takes a diabetes drug every month. Mary is 68 and has a Medicare Advantage plan. The 5 changes: 1) the Medicare Part D 2027 out-of-pocket cap rises from $2,100 to $2,400 and the maximum deductible from $615 to $700, 2) negotiated prices on 15 more drugs start January 1, up to 85% lower (Ozempic, Rybelsus and Wegovy: $959 list price, $274 in 2027), 3) the new GLP-1 Bridge with a $50 copay for a one-month supply, 4) premiums move: the average Medicare Advantage premium drops to $12, stand-alone drug plans about $36, Part B 2027 not announced yet, 5) the trap: discount cards and coupons do not count toward your deductible or your yearly cap. Plus telehealth, electronic prior approvals, the Annual Notice of Change, the key dates and three things to do this week.
