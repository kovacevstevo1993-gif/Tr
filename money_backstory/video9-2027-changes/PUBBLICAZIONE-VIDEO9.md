## Descrizione
2027 Social Security changes, now official: on October 14 the Social Security Administration announced a 3.6% COLA raise for 2027, but that raise is only 1 of 5 new numbers that hit your money in January. We go through all five with the official numbers, step by step.

We follow Frank and Mary, two fictional neighbors. Mary is 67 and fully retired. Frank is 63, claimed early and still works part time. Frank thinks the raise is the only number that matters. Mary found the other four. The five numbers: 1) the 3.6% raise (the average retirement check goes from $2,095 to $2,170, about $75 more a month), 2) SSI (the federal payment goes from $994 to $1,029 for one person), 3) the earnings limit of $25,560 for anyone who claimed before full retirement age and still works, 4) the taxable maximum, up from $184,500 to $192,300, and 5) the price of one work credit, $1,970 in 2027. In Frank's case, the earnings test holds back $5,000 of his benefits in one year. At the end: three things to do this week.
