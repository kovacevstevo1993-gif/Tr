## Titolo
Medicare Advantage Plan Not Renewed for 2027? Why December 7 Is Not Your Last Chance

## Descrizione
Medicare Advantage plan not renewed for 2027? Medicare's own rules say that if you join nothing before your plan ends, you are moved into Original Medicare, and your drug coverage ends with it. But the same rules give you a second window that runs past December 7, and they also set a penalty that is added permanently. In this video you will see the exact dates and numbers, from Medicare.gov and CMS.

We follow one fictional person. Gloria is 71, has a Medicare Advantage plan with drug coverage, and gets a letter in October saying her plan is leaving the Medicare program. What you will learn: 1) what the Plan Non-Renewal Notice is and what Medicare says you must do, 2) the national numbers from CMS: about 5,553 Advantage plans this year and about 5,532 next year, and about 8 in 10 members who can keep their plan at the same or a lower premium, 3) the ordinary window (October 15 to December 7) and the second window (December 8 to the last day of February), 4) when your new plan starts if you ask in December, January or February, 5) what happens if you join nothing: Original Medicare, with no drug plan, 6) the Part D late enrollment penalty: 63 days in a row without drug coverage, 1% of the national base beneficiary premium ($38.99 for 2026) per full month, added permanently, 7) the day count: January 1 to the end of February is 59 days, under the 63-day line, 8) why a plan that is merged into another plan does not get the special window, 9) the blue notice for people with Extra Help, 10) your three steps: find the end date, compare in the Medicare Plan Finder, and what choosing Original Medicare means: a drug plan you add yourself, and the Medigap window of 60 days before to 63 days after.
