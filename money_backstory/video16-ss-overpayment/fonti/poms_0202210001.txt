TN 84 (06-24)
GN 02210.001 
Overpayment Recovery by Benefit Adjustment
CITATIONS:
Section 204(a) of the Social Security Act
20 CFR
 404.502
When we determine that an individual is overpaid, we send a notice requesting full
 and immediate refund and informing the individual of the right to request waiver and
 reconsideration (known as a “due process request”) (see 
GN 02201.009
).
If the 90-day due process recovery period expires without a due process request from
 the individual, and the individual does not refund the overpayment, we recover the
 overpayment by withholding the 10% of the individual’s benefit payment(s) until we
 fully recover the overpayment. The 90-day due process recovery period begins with
 the current operating month (COM), or in other words, the month we establish the overpayment.
 For cases where there has been a due process request, see 
GN 02250.001
 and 
GN 02201.025
.
A. 
What payments to withhold
To recover an overpayment, we withhold the following payments from the overpaid individual,
 representative payee, or any other individual currently receiving benefits on the
 same earnings record on which the overpayment occurred (contingent liability), as
 applicable:
•
Ten percent of (10%)any current and future monthly benefit payment(s);
•
Any underpayment (see 
GN 02301.001A
);
•
Any lump-sum death payment (LSDP);
•
Any misused funds (from the representative payee) that we reissue to an overpaid individual;
 or
•
Any other Social Security administered program payment(s) (i.e. cross program recovery).
B. 
How to withhold the payment
When we have established an overpayment on an individual’s record, and the 90-day
 due process recovery period has elapsed without a due process request from the individual,
 the automated system will begin withholding 10% of the monthly benefit payment until
 we have fully recovered the overpayment (see SM 00610.715C). The automated system
 will withhold any payment (as applicable) listed in 
GN 02210.001A
 from the overpaid individual, the liable representative payee, or a contingently
 liable individual as applicable.
