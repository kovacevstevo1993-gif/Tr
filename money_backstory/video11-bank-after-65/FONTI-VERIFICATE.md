# VIDEO 11 - Never Keep More Than This in One Bank Account After 65 - FONTI UFFICIALI (verificate 20/10/2026)
1. fdic.gov "Understanding Deposit Insurance": "insuring deposits to at least $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category at each FDIC-insured bank"; categorie: single, joint, certain retirement accounts, trust accounts, employee benefit plan, corporation/partnership, government. Joint: "insured separately for your ownership interest up to $250,000 for all of your joint ownership account deposits". NON coperti: stocks, bonds, mutual funds, annuities, life insurance policies, U.S. Treasury securities, municipal securities, crypto assets, safe deposit boxes or their contents. "FDIC deposit insurance doesn't cover default or bankruptcy of any non-FDIC-insured institution". BankFind per verificare.
2. fdic.gov "Financial products insured" + regola trust 01/04/2024: trust (POD/ITF, revocable, irrevocable) = owners x beneficiaries x $250,000, max $1,250,000 per owner per bank (5 o piu' beneficiari). Esempio Frank: POD con 2 figlie = $500,000.
3. fdic.gov brochure "Your Insured Deposits": since the FDIC began operations in 1934, no depositor has ever lost a penny of FDIC-insured deposits. EDIE (edie.fdic.gov) = Electronic Deposit Insurance Estimator.
4. mycreditunion.gov / NCUA: $250,000 per depositor, per federally insured credit union, per ownership category.
5. fdic.gov "Banking With Third-Party Apps" (06/2024): "nonbank companies themselves are never FDIC-insured"; identificare la banca e verificarla con BankFind.
6. consumerfinance.gov "Can a debt collector take my federal benefits...": banca deve guardare 2 mesi indietro; benefici federali con deposito diretto negli ultimi 2 mesi protetti (Social Security, SSI, veterans, federal retirement...). Assegno cartaceo depositato: la banca non deve proteggere 2 mesi, tutto il saldo puo' essere congelato, si deve provare in tribunale. Eccezioni: Social Security puo' essere pignorata per debiti verso il governo (back taxes, federal student loans) e child/spousal support; SSI protetta anche da quelli.
7. consumerfinance.gov (Ask CFPB joint account + advisory 2016 elder financial exploitation): in most circumstances either person on a joint checking account can withdraw money and close the account; rischi: joint owner withdrawing money for own use, exposing funds to creditors of the joint owner; alternative: convenience accounts, powers of attorney, alerts, withdrawal limits.
8. ssa.gov SSI Spotlight on Resources: countable resources max $2,000 individual, $3,000 couple; non contano casa, un veicolo, beni domestici.
9. medicare.gov Medicare Savings Programs 2026: resource limit QMB/SLMB/QI $9,950 individual, $14,910 married couple; alcuni stati non contano certe risorse / limiti piu' alti.
