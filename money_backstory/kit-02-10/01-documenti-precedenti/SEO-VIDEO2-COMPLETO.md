In this video you'll see the 5 retirement mistakes that quietly drain your savings, with real 2026 numbers from Fidelity, Medicare, the IRS and Social Security: planning for too few years, underestimating health care, playing it too safe with cash (inflation), counting on Medicare for long-term care, and forgetting the taxes on your 401(k) and IRA.
