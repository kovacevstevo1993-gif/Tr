## Descrizione
Social Security benefits explained: how your check is really calculated, and the 5 things that decide how big it is, with the exact 2026 numbers from the Social Security Administration.

Most people never learn how Social Security is calculated. In this video we follow Frank and Mary, two retirees with the same wages, through the official formula step by step: the 35-year rule (and what zeros do to your check), the benefit formula with the 2026 bend points, your claiming age (62 vs 67 vs 70), working while you collect (the earnings test), and spouse and survivor benefits. We also look at the question everybody asks: will Social Security still be there?
