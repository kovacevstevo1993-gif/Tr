## Titolo
Can You Retire With $500,000? The Real Monthly Income (Exact Math)

## Descrizione
Can you retire with $500,000? We do the exact math: how much $500,000 really pays every month once Medicare Part B and taxes take their share, and how that compares with what the average household over 65 spends.

We follow Frank and Mary, two fictional neighbors. Both are 67, both are single, both have exactly $500,000 saved and both receive the average Social Security check of $2,071 a month. Frank keeps everything in a traditional 401(k). Mary keeps half in a Roth account. Five numbers decide the answer: 1) how much your savings can safely pay (the 4% rule and Morningstar's 3.9% estimate: about $1,625 a month), 2) what Social Security adds, 3) what Medicare Part B and taxes take before the money reaches you (about $3,493 a month lands in the bank), 4) the gap with average spending and what happens over 25 years, and 5) four things that can change the answer: the age you claim Social Security, an immediate annuity, flexible spending and your retirement date. At the end we put it all on one real budget, so you can redo the math with your own numbers.
