## Titolo
Medicare IRMAA Explained: The $974 Trap at Age 61

## Descrizione
Medicare IRMAA explained: how one extra dollar of income can raise your Medicare premiums by $974 a year, and why age 61 is the most important age to understand it.

Medicare reads your tax return from two years earlier. So the income you report at 63 decides your Part B and Part D premiums at 65. In this video we follow Frank and Mary, both 61 and single, through the 5 reasons the next two years matter: the two-year lookback, the IRMAA income cliffs (with the 2026 brackets), Roth conversions that can make your Social Security taxable, the health insurance cliff before 65, and the 401(k) catch-up window that closes after 63.
