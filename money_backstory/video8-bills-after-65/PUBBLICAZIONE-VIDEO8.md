## Titolo
5 Bills You Don't Have To Pay After 65 (Most Retirees Still Pay Them)

## Descrizione
5 bills you don't have to pay after 65: if you were born before 1962, the law gave you the right to stop, lower or freeze these bills, and most retirees are still paying them. We do the exact math with official numbers.

We follow Frank and Mary, two fictional neighbors. Both are 66, together they receive $3,600 a month from Social Security and take $40,000 a year from Frank's traditional IRA. Five bills decide how much they keep: 1) property tax (homestead exemption, senior exemption and the senior freeze), 2) tax on your Social Security (limits written into law in 1983 and 1993 that never move), 3) the extra standard deduction at 65, 4) the new $6,000 senior deduction (2025 to 2028), and 5) your Medicare Part B premium (Medicare Savings Programs and form SSA-44). Together, bills 3 and 4 cut Frank and Mary's federal income tax by about $1,609 a year. At the end we put it on one list of four calls to make this week.
