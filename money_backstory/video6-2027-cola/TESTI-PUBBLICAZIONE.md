## Titolo
2027 COLA: How Much of Your Social Security Raise You Actually Keep

## Descrizione
2027 COLA explained: the early estimate for the Social Security raise is about 3.5%, but how much of it actually reaches your bank account? Here is the exact math, with official numbers.

Every year Social Security gives retirees a cost-of-living adjustment, known as Cola. In this video we follow Frank and Mary, two retirees with the same average check, through the five things that decide what a raise really leaves you: how Cola is calculated, why it can feel smaller than your bills, what Medicare Part B takes first (and the hold harmless rule), how taxes can change, and the other numbers that move on the same day. The official 2027 number is announced on October 14, so we use the 3.5% estimate as an example and show how to redo the math with the real figure.
